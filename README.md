# BuzzPay

Alby's super simple self-custodial PoS powered by Nostr Wallet Connect.

[Try it now!](https://getalby.github.io/pos/)

Easily save to your homescreen as a PWA and share with your team with a single link or QR code.

## Development

`yarn install`
`yarn dev`

## Contributing

We aim to keep BuzzPay as simple as possible. For more advanced features or customizations consider making a fork (such as [Sats Factory](https://github.com/dmnyc/pos)) and feel free to let us know about it.
